## Overview

Electrical Engineer and R&D manager with over a decade of experience in industrial innovation, applied robotics, and intelligent systems.

* **R&D Leadership:** R&D Manager at the SENAI Institute of Technology in Operational Efficiency (ISTEO), leading research teams, applied technology consulting, and industrial infrastructure projects.
* **Academic Research:** M.Sc. candidate in Electrical Engineering at Universidade Federal do Espírito Santo (UFES), working on Multi-Camera Programmable Intelligent Spaces (PrIS) for extended perception in autonomous mobile robot navigation.
* **Programs & Consortia:** Collaborating researcher in the National Institute of Science and Technology for Digital Transformation (INCT IN-FOTON) and the A2AI project (Human-Robot-Environment Cooperation in Intelligent Spaces). Technical co-creator of the SESI SENAI AI Tower.
* **Intellectual Property:** Co-inventor of software registrations and utility models at INPI for offshore dynamic positioning systems.
